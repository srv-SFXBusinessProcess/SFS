Why sell with Screwfix

A trusted route to the people who buy for the job

Extend your reach without opening a new channel from scratch. We bring the customers, the brand and the platform; you bring the range.

Reach trade customers

Get your products seen by professional tradespeople and committed DIYers who come to Screwfix ready to buy.

Sell under a trusted name

Benefit from the confidence customers place in Screwfix, with a consistent checkout, payments and customer service experience.

Fast, guided onboarding

A dedicated onboarding team and simple product data templates help you go from approval to live listings quickly.

Flexible fulfilment

Dispatch from your own stock to customers’ homes or to Screwfix stores, or hold consignment stock with us and let Screwfix fulfil.

Insight to help you grow

Track sales, stock and customer feedback in a vendor portal, so you can see what’s working and where to expand your range.

Clear, simple commercials
